## Project Overview

Aero Carrier was an air-ground integrated drone vehicle for a ball-transport task. The system had to move from the start area, collect balls, and deliver them to a target area under size and time constraints.

The final version was not the first version. During the midterm stage, the team faced barometer failure, rollover, structural damage, and unstable task execution. The important part was the recovery process: analyze the system, revise the mechanism, tune the control strategy, and test again.

## My Responsibility

My assigned work focused on electrical control design, tuning tests, and mechatronic system handling. In practice, that meant helping the flight system, ground pickup mechanism, and task sequence behave as one system rather than separate pieces.

## Lessons

This project is valuable because it shows a complete iteration loop:

- define the task and constraints,
- build a prototype,
- expose failure through testing,
- identify whether the issue is mechanical, electrical, control-related, or procedural,
- modify the design and test again.

That loop is exactly the kind of engineering rhythm I want this portfolio to preserve.
